# NX2Saria 0.26.0-rc.2 in detail

The log lines and tests behind the [0.26.0-rc.2 release notes](../releases/v0.26.0-rc.2.md). How to install or update
is in [the install guide](../install.md). It is the second release candidate of 0.26.0, for launchers on the Preview
channel, and has everything of [0.26.0-rc.1](v0.26.0-rc.1.md) plus the fix below.

## NVIDIA's low latency on a driver from 2023 (#117)

With low latency on (`low_latency = 1` or `2`) and frame generation off, 0.26.0-rc.1 ended on the 600th present with
`FATAL: access violation (0xc0000005) ... (nvoglv64.dll+...), reading address 0x10` on an NVIDIA driver from 2023. The
fault is older than 0.26.0: the path had not changed since 0.24.0. `low_latency` is 0 by default.

- The cause: every 600 presents the game asks the driver for its frame timings (`vkGetLatencyTimingsNV`). Revision 1
  of `VK_NV_low_latency2` took that call with four arguments, revision 2 and later take three, and the game used the
  three-argument form without reading which revision the driver lists. A revision 1 driver then read an argument
  nobody passed. NVIDIA's Windows drivers list revision 1 up to 546.31.
- Now the game reads the revision when it makes the device. On revision 1 it does not ask for the timings; the sleep,
  the markers and the frame limit run as on any revision. On revision 2 and later nothing changes.
- Start: `lowlat: NVIDIA's low latency through VK_NV_low_latency2 revision N (presents carry the frame's number): on
  (NX2SARIA_LOW_LATENCY=1)`, and on revision 1 the line goes on with `; the driver's frame timings are not asked for:
  this revision gives them through another form of the call, so the frame lines come without the driver's report`.
- The `lowlat: frame N:` line of a revision 1 driver says once `no report from the driver: revision 1 of
  VK_NV_low_latency2 gives its frame timings through another form of the call, which is not made (said once)`.
- How it was found: the crash log sent through the new offer of 0.26.0-rc.1, and the build's map, which put the fault
  in `vk_lowlat_present_end` on the present thread.
- Not tried on an NVIDIA card yet: the test runs the path over a made-up driver of each revision.
- Test: `tests/lowlat_revision_check.py`. Notes: [code/pacing.md](../code/pacing.md), "The extension's revisions".

## Seen on Windows with 0.26.0-rc.1

On a laptop with a GeForce RTX 3050 6GB: the update with its partial rebuild (3.6 minutes), *Quit Game* from the title
screen (`quit_menu: title: Quit Game chosen; closing the game`, a normal end), the crash log offer after a real crash
(shown, sent, folders written short) and no offer after a normal end. *Quit Game* from the pause menu was not used.
