# NX2Saria 0.18.0 in detail

The option names, log lines and measurements behind the [0.18.0 release notes](../releases/v0.18.0.md).
How to install or update is in [the install guide](../install.md).

## New

- **The HUD and the menus on an ultrawide picture** (game 1.1.1, #9). Two settings in `nx2saria.ini` `[display]`, both
  on the *Screen* tab under *Widescreen*, read at start. Both do nothing at 16:9. [docs/wide-hud.md](../wide-hud.md)
  has the design, every moved pane and the checks.
  - `wide_hud = center | edges` (default `center`, the game's layout in the middle 16:9, as before). `edges` moves the
    hearts, magic, D-pad guide and Stone of Agony to the top left, the item slot and sub-items to the top right, and
    the minimap with its icons, the key counter and the rupee pop-up to the right edge. The pause map stays in place.
  - `wide_menus = full | center` (default `full`). `full` lays the pause, map, system, item wheel and other menus out
    over the whole picture; before 0.18.0 they stayed in the middle 16:9. `center` keeps them in the middle 16:9 with
    black sides while a pause or system menu, the loading screen or a movie is up.
- **How it works:**
  - `runtime/wide_hud.c` edits the Common and Title layout archives while the RomFS comes up, after the action
    prompt's edit of the same archive. Every value is checked against the 1.1.1 files before it is written. A layout
    that differs, such as a mod's, keeps its own layout, with a log line.
  - The pause menu's painted backdrop (`PlusMenu_00` `P_MenuBg_00`) is cut into 7 pieces that move with their
    columns, so the painted slots stay under the icons at any shape. The archive grows by 18,432 bytes, and the
    resource size table's entry for it is raised to match. If the entry cannot be raised, the archive is made again
    without the growth.
  - `wide_menus = center`: the new patch set `wide_bars` hands the runtime the menu background and loading fade
    screens, and the present clears the sides while one is up (`runtime/wide_bars.c`, `gfx_present.c`).
  - Tools: `tools/layout_dump.py`, `tools/layout_preview.py` (textured before and after previews) and
    `tools/layout_textures.py`.

## Its log (`logs/nx2saria.log`)

- `widescreen: menus full: ...` or `widescreen: menus center: ...`, with what the setting does for this picture, or
  that the picture is 16:9 and there is nothing to keep.
- `wide hud: center|edges, picture WxH (ratio): ...`, with what each layout moves, or that no pane moves.
- `wide menus: full, picture WxH: ...` and one line per layout with its moved fields.
- `wide hud: the layout archive edited (...), N fields and M new panes, the archive A -> B bytes`, then
  `RomFS: resource size table: ... raised X -> Y` for the larger archive.

## Tested

- `tests/wide_hud_check.py --romfs`, `tests/wide_hud_title_check.py --romfs`, `tests/action_prompt_check.py --romfs`,
  `tests/wide_bars_check.py`, `tests/layout_tools_check.py`, `tests/cfg_rows_check.py` and `tests/tracker_api_check.py`
  pass, the `--romfs` ones on the game's own files.
- On the Linux bench (RX 6700, RADV), headless with scripted input: 21:9 with `edges` and `full` (pause pages, map,
  gameplay) and 32:9 with `edges` and `center` (black sides on pause, map, loading and a movie). The owner also played
  it on Linux.
- Before the release, two runs with the default settings: at 16:9 no layout is edited; at 21:9 the menus are laid out
  over the whole picture, the archive grows from 9,927,489 to 9,945,921 bytes and its resource size entry is raised,
  with a mod's resource size table in place. Settings and saves unchanged.
- Not covered by a run yet: the pause in `full` at 32:9, page changes in motion, the rupee pop-up and item wheel in
  play, and HDR or frame generation together with the black sides.
- The Windows build was built and checked by the release workflow. 0.18.0 stayed a pre-release; its changes reached
  the stable release with 0.18.1 on 2026-11-18.

The ultrawide HUD and menu approach was informed by ideas from the Triforce project; none of its code is used here.
