# NX2Saria 0.22.0 in detail

The stable release after 0.21.2, made from the same code as
[0.22.0-rc.1](https://github.com/Larbaco/NX2Saria/releases/tag/v0.22.0-rc.1), which was played on Linux and on
Windows. Its changes, with their log lines and tests, are in [0.22.0-rc.1 in detail](v0.22.0-rc.1.md):

- holding the preloaded neighbour map in towns such as the Market (#61);
- the camera's clearance at 16:9 on wider pictures (#62);
- replay timing for profiling, `NX2SARIA_REPLAY_PHASES=1` (#65).

How to install or update is in [the install guide](../install.md), and how to check a downloaded file in
[Verify your download](../install.md#verify-your-download).

## Played on Linux (AMD RX 6700, RADV, 3440x1440 at 21:9)

- `patches:` showed `changebank_hold on` and `camera_clearance on`.
- Between Hyrule Field and Hyrule Castle the game's choice of neighbour map changed 96 times; 63 choices were held
  back and 31 of them came back to the held map within the 2 s, each a cancelled load and its reload avoided.
- `camera clearance: this session`: 19944 reads at `main+0x71a844` and 203 at `main+0x71b624` made 16:9, so the
  second site, which did not run on the bench, runs in play.
- About 10 minutes of play, a clean exit, no fatal error and no GPU timeout in the kernel log.

## Played on Windows (laptop with an NVIDIA RTX 3050, 6 GiB)

- The first update after 0.21.2 found NVIDIA's shader cache in the game's folder: `library warm: NVIDIA's shader
  disk cache folder ... nvidia-cache holds 1.51 GB in 2 files`, and the driver's cache had the prepared libraries
  (12 of 12 sampled built in under 15 ms).
- `changebank hold: this session`: 42 choices held back, 26 back to the held map within the hold.
- `camera clearance: this session`: 6910 reads made 16:9.
- No fatal error and no lost graphics device. Compared with a session of 0.21.2-rc.2 with the same settings, frames
  over 100 ms went from 15 to 7 a minute in play.

## Known

- On that laptop play stays bound by the replay on the CPU (more than 95% of the frames over 25 ms) and by free
  memory (400 to 600 MB of 16 GB free in play); with frame generation at 3x the frame rate shown is about three
  times the game's. This release does not change that.
