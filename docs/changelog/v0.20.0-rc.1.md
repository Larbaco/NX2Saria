# NX2Saria 0.20.0-rc.1 in detail

The option names, log lines and tests behind the [0.20.0-rc.1 release notes](../releases/v0.20.0-rc.1.md).
How to install or update is in [the install guide](../install.md).

0.20.0-rc.1 is the first release candidate of 0.20.0, for launchers on the Preview channel. It follows 0.19.1, the
stable release, and adds features and new defaults: quality presets, dynamic resolution on 1.1.1, and taller picture
shapes. The details of each are in [docs/presets.md](../presets.md) and the pull requests.

## Quality presets and the scene below the picture's size (#33)

- New setting `preset` (`recommended|low|medium|high|ultra`, default `recommended`). A preset is a layer of defaults:
  what the player sets (environment, `nx2saria.ini`) and the Steam Deck profile always win over it, and
  `nx2saria.ini` is never written by the detection. Its state is kept in `preset-state.txt` beside the game.
- **High is the game as shipped** and sets nothing. Recommended is worked out at every start from a Vulkan probe and
  the CPU and RAM:
  - a handheld, an integrated GPU or a GPU without mesh shaders gets Low;
  - otherwise video memory decides: under 6 GiB Low, under 8 Medium, under 16 High, Ultra from 16;
  - RAM under 10240 MiB caps it at Low, under 14336 MiB at Medium, and 4 logical processors or fewer cap it at Medium.
- The tiers set `scene_share`, `game_res`, `standin_filter`, `aniso`, `foliage`, `texture_detail`, `fps_limit` (only
  on an iGPU or a handheld), `shadow_pcf`, and DLSS or FSR 4 modes on the cards that have them. No tier turns frame
  generation on or uses FSR 3.1. The table is in [docs/presets.md](../presets.md).
- Menu, Settings > Screen > Picture: a *Quality preset* row with a *(custom)* mark, a box that lists the hand-set
  options a choice hands back to the preset, and a one-time notice when a PC recommended below High uses it. The full
  performance overlay shows the preset.
- *Scene resolution* row (33 to 100 %, live): the 3D scene's share of the picture while neither DLSS nor FSR runs.
  The HUD and menus stay at the picture's size. On 1.1.1 `render_scale` and `output_res` do nothing; the docs say so.
- Log: `preset: hardware: GPU ... probe N ms`, `preset: recommended <tier>: <reason>`, `preset: in use <tier> (...)`,
  one `preset:   KEY = value (...)` per key it sets, `preset: <old> -> <new>: KEY before -> after` on a change in the
  menu, and `preset: notice shown: ...`.

## Dynamic resolution on 1.1.1 (#34)

- `dyn_res = 1` now reaches the scene on 1.1.1. With DLSS, FSR 3.1 or FSR 4 it steps the mode; without an upscaler it
  steps `scene_share` itself, down to the new `dyn_res_min` (default 0.5, 0.33 to 1), never above the share the
  player or the preset chose.
- Steps cannot ping-pong: down at 8 % over the target, up under 75 % of it, at least 1 s between steps and 3 s from a
  step down to a step up; a step up undone within 5 s doubles that wait, up to 48 s.
- `dyn_res_target = 0` (automatic) is 0.9 times the frame limit's frame time: 15.0 ms at 60 fps.
- Menu, Screen tab: *Dynamic resolution* and *Minimum scene resolution*. The presets do not turn it on.
- Log: `resolution: dynamic resolution on (...)`, then `resolution: dynamic resolution: GPU X ms a frame against Y ms:
  scene share A -> B`, and `resolution: dynamic resolution has no room: ...`, each limited per run.

## 16:10 and 3:2 pictures (#40)

- `game_aspect` takes `window`, `16:10` and `3:2` besides 16:9 and the wider shapes (menu: *Game aspect*). A taller
  shape keeps 16:9's horizontal view and shows more above and below (Vert+); the picture and the scene grow in
  height, for example 2560x1600 and 1920x1200, with about 34 MiB more texture heap.
- The HUD stays whole in the centred 16:9 band; whole-picture backgrounds, the pause menu's background, event and
  loading fades and movies fill the full height; the save picture keeps its centred 16:9 crop.
- The Steam Deck profile already sets `game_aspect = window`, so a Deck at 1280x800 gets a 16:10 picture.
- 16:9 and the wider shapes are unchanged; the `wide_*` checks cover 16:10, 3:2, 16:9 and 21:9.
- Thanks to the player who asked for it in issue #27.

## Tested

- Bench (Linux, RX 6700): the presets' tiers measured in Kokiri Forest and Hyrule Field, dynamic resolution on each
  tier with and without FSR 3.1, and 1280x800 captures of an event fade, the pause background and a loading fade.
- Tests: `preset_check`, `dynres_check`, `cfg_parse_check`, `cfg_rows_check`, the `wide_*` checks and
  `docs_i18n check`.
- Windows builds of each change by GitHub Actions.
- Not tried yet: any of this on Windows, a PC limited by its GPU with dynamic resolution, and a real 16:10 screen
  (Steam Deck, Legion Go, a 1920x1200 monitor), including game over and the cinema bars of events.
