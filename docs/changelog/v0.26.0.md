# NX2Saria 0.26.0 in detail

The stable release after 0.25.0, made from the same code as
[0.26.0-rc.2](https://github.com/Larbaco/NX2Saria/releases/tag/v0.26.0-rc.2). Its changes, with their log lines and
tests, are in [0.26.0-rc.1 in detail](v0.26.0-rc.1.md) and [0.26.0-rc.2 in detail](v0.26.0-rc.2.md):

- *Quit Game* in the game's own menus on game 1.1.1;
- the launcher's offer to send the game's log after a crash;
- the mesh fallback on Linux for Radeon RX 6000 and newer with Mesa 26.2 or 26.3;
- NVIDIA's low latency on drivers that list revision 1 of `VK_NV_low_latency2` (#117).

How to install or update is in [the install guide](../install.md), and how to check a downloaded file in
[Verify your download](../install.md#verify-your-download). What a session report and a crash log hold is in
[telemetry.md](../telemetry.md).

## Played on Windows (GeForce RTX 3050 6GB laptop, driver 546.18)

- The update from 0.26.0-rc.1 on the Preview channel: `installing 0.26.0-rc.2`, `compile: 10 s`, `shaders: 18 s`,
  `finished in 0.5 min`, `job update: ok`.
- The setting that crashed 0.26.0-rc.1, `low_latency = 1` with frame generation off. The driver lists revision 1:
  `lowlat: NVIDIA's low latency through VK_NV_low_latency2 revision 1 (...): on (NX2SARIA_LOW_LATENCY=1); the driver's
  frame timings are not asked for: this revision gives them through another form of the call, so the frame lines come
  without the driver's report`.
- The 600th present, where the candidate before ended: `lowlat: frame 600: the game's own limiter holds the frame
  limit; 599 frame starts held 5.61 ms on average ...; no report from the driver: revision 1 of VK_NV_low_latency2
  gives its frame timings through another form of the call, which is not made (said once)`. Later, in play: `lowlat:
  frame 4200: the driver holds frames to 16.667 ms; 3597 frame starts held 4.76 ms on average ...; 0 sleeps not ended
  in time`. No `FATAL`; the session ended with `end normal` when the game was restarted from its settings menu.
- A second session with DLSS (`dlaa: DLSS feature created at 854x360 -> 2560x1080`) also passed the 600th present.
- That computer's memory was nearly full while the game loaded (`system memory critical, 0.6% free`, 16 GB of RAM).
  The game went on.

## Played on Linux (AMD Radeon RX 6700, Mesa 25.2.8)

- The update from 0.25.0-rc.1 on the Preview channel, with its partial rebuild, then `version: NX2Saria 0.26.0-rc.2`.
- *Quit Game* from the pause menu, by hand: `quit_menu: pause: the Title Screen confirmation opens with three buttons
  (Title Screen, Cancel, Quit Game)`, once `the confirmation closed without Quit Game (Title Screen or Cancel)`, back
  on the title screen `the fourth button's pane shown`, then `quit_menu: pause: Quit Game chosen; closing the game`
  and `session summary written ... (1444 bytes, end normal)` after a session of 20 minutes.

## Known

- *Quit Game* from the pause menu was not used on Windows; from the title screen it was, with 0.26.0-rc.1.
- The low latency fix was seen on one NVIDIA driver, 546.18. On a revision 1 driver the `lowlat: frame N:` lines
  come without the driver's own timings.
- Mesa 26.2 and 26.3 were not played with this release by the project: the mesh fallback there is as tested for
  0.26.0-rc.1.
- Low latency is off by default (`low_latency = 0`).
