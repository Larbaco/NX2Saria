- New since rc.2, experimental and off by default: a settings tab in the game's own pause menu, after the *Menu* tab, for playing with only a controller. `pause_settings_tab = 1` in `nx2saria.ini` turns it on (game 1.1.1); R on the *Menu* tab opens it.
- The tab holds the picture settings for now (frame limit, VSync, upscaler, frame generation, sharpening, anisotropic filtering), with the same "Apply your changes?" box. The settings menu and its shortcut stay as they are.
- From rc.2: `foliage_distance` in `nx2saria.ini`, triggers that count after a second at rest, and the notice for Linux kernels older than 6.7.
- From rc.1: fixes for FSR 4 frame generation and for DLSS with DLSS frame generation on Linux, a crash report that says the graphics card and settings, and the game's crash line always written whole.
- This update rebuilds a small part of the game; the long step, the game's own code, is not repeated.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
Pre-release: the third candidate of 0.27.0, for the Preview channel. The second candidate was installed, built, run and crashed on purpose by an automatic test on Linux. The new tab was tried with scripted input on Linux only: not with a real controller, not at a real ultrawide size and not on Windows. DLSS and DLAA on Windows were not yet played with this build.
