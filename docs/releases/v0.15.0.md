- Smoother play while shaders are still being prepared after an update or a graphics driver update.
- The pipeline cache no longer keeps growing with data the game stopped using.
- Fewer crashes and wrong pictures when the window changes size or the graphics driver refuses a setting.
- For testers: `boot_map` starts the game straight in an area, without the title screen.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
No shader is translated again: the shader translator is the one 0.14.0 has.
