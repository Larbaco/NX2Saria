- The Linux AppImage now installs on Ubuntu 24.04, Linux Mint 22 and other systems that package no SDL3: it brings its own.
- On those systems it asks only for packages that exist there, and builds with their default compiler (clang 18).
- On Arch, Fedora and other systems with SDL3, the game builds with the system's SDL3, as before.
- Windows: nothing changes for you in this update.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
Pre-release, as 0.18.0: its ultrawide HUD and menus have not been played on Windows yet.
