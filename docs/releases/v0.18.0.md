- On ultrawide screens (21:9, 32:9), the pause, map and system menus now fill the whole screen.
- Prefer them in the middle with black sides? Pick *Black bars* under *Menus* on the Screen tab (`wide_menus = center`).
- New: *Screen edges* under *HUD position* moves the hearts, items and minimap out to the corners (`wide_hud = edges`).
- At 16:9 nothing changes, and mods that replace the game's menus keep their own layout.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
Superseded by 0.18.1, which has all of 0.18.0: update to it instead.
