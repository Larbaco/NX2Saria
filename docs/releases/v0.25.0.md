- New: when the video memory stays full and the game slows down, a notice on screen says so and names what to lower (Resolution, the Upscaling mode, Frame generation). `vram_warning = 0` in `nx2saria.ini` turns it off.
- In Settings, the "Apply your changes?" box says when a change uses more video memory while it is already tight or full, with the numbers.
- On cards with less than 8 GiB, the same box says when a change goes past what the Recommended preset uses for the card. Neither note stops you from applying.
- The Apply box shows each change on its own line: "(after restart)" no longer breaks in the middle of a word in Portuguese and Russian.
- The launcher asks about session reports as soon as an update or the first installation finishes, not one start later. There the question waits for your answer, without the 30 second countdown.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
The same code as 0.25.0-rc.1, played on Linux until a 10 GiB card really filled up and the notice came. On Windows it ran on two players' PCs that send session reports, not on the project's own.
