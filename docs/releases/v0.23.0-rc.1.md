- Faster on Intel graphics and older AMD cards on Windows: where the game draws the world the safe way, it now batches that work, about 2 to 3 more frames a second on the bench (`mesh_fallback_batch = 0` in `nx2saria.ini` turns it off).
- The launcher closes at once when you log out or shut down the PC, instead of holding them up.
- Settings still spelled with the old `NX2NAVI_` names keep working, and the log now names the current `NX2SARIA_` one.
- Code cheats and ExeFS patches you turn on are built in by the next Play from the launcher; the log says so.
- Each release now lists every third-party part it ships and carries their licence texts; the Linux AppImage drops a Python module the game never used.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
