- New: *Quit Game* in the game's own menus on game 1.1.1: a fourth entry on the title screen, and a third button in the question the pause menu's *Title Screen* opens. `quit_menu = 0` in `nx2saria.ini` turns it off.
- New: after the game crashes, the launcher offers to send the start and the end of the game's log. Nothing is sent unless you choose *Send the Log*, and *See What Is Sent* shows the exact text first.
- Linux, Radeon RX 6000 and newer with Mesa 26.2 or 26.3: the game no longer ends with "the GPU device was lost" within seconds; it draws through the mesh fallback there until Mesa's fix is released.
- The crash log offer can be turned off in the launcher: Settings, Session Reports, *Ask After a Crash*.
- This update compiles part of the game again, a few minutes, because *Quit Game* changes three of the game's functions.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
Pre-release: the first candidate of 0.26.0, for the Preview channel. *Quit Game* was tried on Linux with scripted input, not yet on Windows or with a real controller, and the crash log offer was not yet sent by a released launcher.
