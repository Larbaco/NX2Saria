- NVIDIA on Windows: the game keeps its shader cache in its own folder, so updates no longer rebuild every shader (it took 15 to 30 minutes on a laptop).
- Intel graphics: the fog no longer shows holes on Arc cards; the game draws the world the safe way on Intel by itself.
- Fixed: a graphics card chosen by an older version only by its position no longer puts the game on an integrated card when a dedicated one is there.
- The logs give the local time of slow frames and crashes, the PC's free memory each minute, and which card the shaders were prepared for.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
On NVIDIA, this update still prepares the shaders once, into the new folder; the next ones take seconds.
