- Linux: the AppImage installs the full launcher, the same one as on Windows, and puts NX2Saria in the Applications menu.
- Linux: the launcher updates itself in the background, Play starts the game, and Add to Steam works, the Steam Deck too.
- Linux: the launcher finds your game dump on other drives and offers to unlock udmabuf for a faster game where the system blocks it.
- Fixed: rare timing faults between the game's threads, found by a new checker on the test bench.
- Fixed: Apply no longer lists a setting that a quality preset gives back at the same value as a change.
- launcher.log says how the window was closed, each setting you change, and why Play did not start the game.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
On a PC with two graphics cards, choose the card in the menu again (or Auto) if the game feels slow or stops when a save loads.
