- The mouse works in the game's own menus: hover to pick, left click to confirm, right click to go back, wheel to scroll.
- After a menu the mouse turns the camera again, and a gamepad press hides the pointer. Off: `menu_cursor = 0`.
- The gyro learns its drift whenever the controller rests, so the aim no longer creeps. Off: `gyro_calibration = 0`.
- New gyro options on the Controls tab: dead zone, and separate sensitivity and invert for turn and tilt.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
Pre-release: a test build of the menu mouse and the gyro options. The stable release stays 0.18.1.
