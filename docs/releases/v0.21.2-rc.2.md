- NVIDIA on Windows: the game keeps its shader cache in its own folder, so an update may no longer rebuild every shader (15 to 30 minutes on a laptop).
- Intel graphics: the fog no longer shows holes on Arc cards; the game now draws the world the safe way on Intel by itself.
- The log says where NVIDIA's shader cache is kept and how much it holds before each shader preparation.
- `nvidia_cache_dir = 0` and `mesh_fallback = 0` in `nx2saria.ini` turn the two changes off.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are.
Pre-release: the second candidate of 0.21.2, for the Preview channel. On NVIDIA, the first update after this one still prepares the shaders once, into the new folder.
