- The mouse works in the game's own menus (not yet on the tab row at the top), and the gyro no longer lets the aim creep.
- Xbox, PlayStation, Steam Deck and keyboard button prompts work again: pick them under Controls, "Button prompts".
- FSR 3.1 frame generation keeps working when the scene is smaller than the picture, and Intel Arc fog has no holes.
- The game's build starts each compile by the free memory, so installing and updating no longer run the PC out of it.
- This update compiles the whole game once, which takes about as long as the compile step of a first install.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
This update includes 0.18.2 to 0.18.5, which came out as pre-releases.
