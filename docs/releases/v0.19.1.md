- Updates no longer spend hours preparing shaders on some NVIDIA PCs: huge shaders the game never uses are skipped.
- If the graphics driver gets stuck on one shader for 5 minutes, the update finishes and the game does the rest later.
- The launcher's What's New shows the whole notes, with a scroll bar: mouse wheel, PgUp/PgDn or the right stick.
- Tip for NVIDIA cards: a larger Shader Cache Size in the NVIDIA Control Panel keeps shaders between updates.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
This update already prepares its shaders the faster way.
