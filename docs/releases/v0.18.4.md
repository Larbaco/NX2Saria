- Xbox, PlayStation, Steam Deck and keyboard button prompts work again: pick them under Controls, "Button prompts".
- The update draws those prompts once on your PC, which adds about half a minute.
- If the prompt files are ever missing, the menu now says to update the game or rebuild it in the launcher.
- The game's log says where its button prompts came from, or why there are none.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are when you update.
Pre-release, as 0.18.3: its new build has not yet installed the game on a Windows PC.
