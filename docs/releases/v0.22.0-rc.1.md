- Fewer stutters in busy towns such as the Market: turning around no longer makes the game restart loading the next area each time (most help on PCs with few CPU cores).
- On screens wider than 16:9 the camera stays close in crawlspaces and tight passages, as it does at 16:9.
- `changebank_hold = 0` and `camera_clearance_fix = 0` in `nx2saria.ini` turn the two changes off, and the log counts what each one did.
- For testers: `NX2SARIA_REPLAY_PHASES=1` times each step of the game's drawing work, to find where the CPU time goes.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are.
Pre-release: the first candidate of 0.22.0, for the Preview channel. Not tried yet on a PC with few cores, where the Market change should help most.
