- Fixed: the HUD (hearts, magic, items, minimap) was missing in 0.20.0-rc.1 at every screen shape. It is back.
- Quality presets: Recommended, Low, Medium, High and Ultra, in Settings > Screen > Picture. High is the game as before.
- Slower PCs can draw the 3D scene below the picture's size (Scene resolution); the HUD and text stay sharp.
- Dynamic resolution now works on game 1.1.1, with or without DLSS or FSR: the scene shrinks only when frames would drop.
- 16:10 and 3:2 screens (Steam Deck, Legion Go, 16:10 monitors): Game aspect "Window shape" fills them, no black bars.

Your saves, mods and settings stay where they are. Anything you set by hand wins over a preset.
Pre-release: the second release candidate for the Preview channel. Not tried on Windows yet: presets, 16:10.
